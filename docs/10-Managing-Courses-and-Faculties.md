# 10 — Managing courses and faculties

Course and faculty information is embedded in the institution's MongoDB document. You manage it from the institution editor; there are no separate course/faculty collection pages.

## Add a course

1. Open **Admin → Institutions**, then edit the institution.
2. Open **Courses & faculty** and choose **Add course**.
3. Enter a course name. Add its category, degree/qualification, duration, department, eligibility, admission requirements and description.
4. Enter a tuition fee and available seat count only when you know them. Fees must be non-negative numbers.
5. Leave **Visible publicly** selected if visitors should see the course. At least one course must be public before the institution can be published.
6. Choose **Save changes**. Reopen the institution to verify the course stayed in the record.

The administrator catalog includes course category labels. The current course editor accepts a category label as text, so use the catalog spelling consistently.

## Add a faculty member

1. In the same tab, find **Faculty profiles** and choose **Add faculty**.
2. Enter the person's name. Add designation, department, qualification, years of experience and biography if known.
3. Save the institution before uploading a photo.
4. Use the faculty photo upload box and wait for its preview.
5. Choose **Save changes** and confirm the faculty information remains.

The separate **Departments** area lets you enter department labels. Type a name and press Enter or the plus button; use the small close icon to remove a label before saving.

## Facilities

1. Open the **Facilities** tab.
2. Select a common facility or add a custom facility with a short label and description.
3. Add no more than eight photos to a facility.
4. Save changes. The facility code must be unique within this institution.

## Fees and admissions

Enter fee rows with a label, amount, currency, period and notes. Mark the information as institution-provided only when it came from the institution; otherwise keep it marked unverified. Add admission procedure, application deadline, scholarships, financial aid and admissions contact details in the corresponding fields. Review the public institution page after saving to confirm visitor-visible information is accurate.
